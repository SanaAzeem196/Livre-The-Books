About Livre

A little place for big ideas.

Livre is a simple home for readers who believe that every book has somewhere worth taking you. From timeless classics and gripping mysteries to inspiring memoirs and thoughtful works of science, our collection brings different worlds together under one roof.

Our goal is simple: make discovering and ordering great books easy, welcoming, and enjoyable.

Why Livre?

Curated: A collection of books worth knowing.

Simple: Browse, add to your cart, and order with ease.

For Readers: Stories and ideas for every kind of reader.
